## Official/public sources referenced by the application

- Texas Department of Insurance (TDI): homeowners market overview, county loss data, home insurance guide, home/flood/wind guidance, Consumer Bills of Rights, claims guidance and HelpInsure context.
- Texas Division of Emergency Management (TDEM): preparedness, damage-reporting and recovery context.
- Texas Water Development Board (TWDB): 2024 State Flood Plan and regional/state flood-planning context.
- Texas A&M Forest Service: wildfire preparedness, Firewise USA and home-hardening guidance.
- FEMA / OpenFEMA: disaster declarations.
- National Flood Insurance Program / FloodSmart: flood-policy and waiting-period context.
- NOAA / National Weather Service: weather alert API.

Official-source content remains subject to the issuing agency's terms, notices and current version. This bundle generally stores short factual summaries and links rather than copying long source documents.
